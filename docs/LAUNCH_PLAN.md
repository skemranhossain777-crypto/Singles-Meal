
# Singles Meal Launch Plan

## Stage 1: Validate operations (weeks 1–2)
- Pick one delivery zone and define order cutoffs.
- Confirm kitchen capacity, packaging, food safety and cold-chain processes.
- Cost every recipe and establish gross margin.
- Validate sample menus and nutrition with a qualified professional.
- Interview 15–20 target customers and 3–5 corporate prospects.

## Stage 2: Pilot MVP (weeks 3–6)
- Launch 15–20 meals and a limited weekly subscription.
- Take a controlled number of orders per delivery slot.
- Manually verify all allergies with kitchen staff.
- Measure on-time delivery, waste, repeat orders, complaints and contribution margin.

## Stage 3: Public launch (weeks 7–10)
- Add payment-provider webhooks, customer support workflow and refunds.
- Add kitchen production reports and inventory controls.
- Introduce referral offers and corporate lunch pilots.
- Expand delivery coverage only when service levels remain stable.

## Success metrics
- On-time delivery >= 95% target
- Order accuracy >= 98% target
- Food waste tracked by ingredient and batch
- Contribution margin positive after food, packaging, payment and delivery costs
- Subscription renewal and cohort retention
- Customer complaint resolution time
